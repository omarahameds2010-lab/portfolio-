01The story

Built in public.
Learned by doing.

I didn't start with a perfect plan. I started by building, shipping, and asking better questions.

My first projects were small experiments. Then came real clients, a national competition, and AI systems that had to prove they worked. Each step made one thing clear: I want to build technology that holds up outside the demo.

Today I work across the stack — from database-level security and APIs to the interface people actually touch. I lead when the team needs direction, document what I learn, and keep shipping.

The goal is not to look busy. It is to make things that work.

02Selected work

01 — Flagship platform

Secure Multi-Tenant E-Learning Platform

A SaaS learning platform designed around strict tenant isolation and defense in depth: PostgreSQL Row-Level Security with FORCE RLS, a non-superuser app user, host-based tenant routing, and a seven-layer authorization engine covering tenant checks, RBAC, ownership, entitlements, feature flags, tenant policies, and plan limits.
